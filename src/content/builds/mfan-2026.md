MFan 2026 began as an easier way for a group of friends to make match predictions without messy chat threads or manual spreadsheets. The result is a mobile-first app for voting, tracking results and a bit of friendly rivalry.

## What I built

![MFan 2026 leaderboard screenshot](/images/mfan-leaderboard.webp)

![MFan 2026 league insights screenshot](/images/mfan-insights.webp)

The app uses Google Apps Script, HTML5, CSS and a Google Sheet backend. Its leaderboard tracks virtual earnings and accuracy; the Battle Grid compares everyone's picks. Dedicated players can vote, while guests get a view-only experience.

I acted as the product architect and used AI tools to help write, debug and improve the code. The project went through 94 releases during the tournament. The [release log and app terms and privacy policy](../../mfan-2026/) remain available at the old address.
