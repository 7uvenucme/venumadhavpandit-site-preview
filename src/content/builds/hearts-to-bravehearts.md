A collaboration involving the Indian Army, India Post, Mahindra Auto, Faujiana and Autocar honoured 25 years of Kargil Vijay Diwas.

The photograph above was taken at Kargil War Memorial in Dras, Ladakh. Veterans of the 1999 conflict, officers and teams from Mahindra, Autocar and Faujiana appear with two of the 20+ Mahindra SUVs that travelled more than 10,000 km across India.
